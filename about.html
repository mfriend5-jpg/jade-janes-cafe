About Jade & Janes

Welcome to Jade & Janes Café & Bakery! We're a sister-owned café with a passion for fresh sourdough, handcrafted coffee, and creating a welcoming place for our community.

Our Story

Jade & Janes started with two sisters who have always been incredibly close. Growing up, we shared a love for coffee and freshly baked treats, something we inherited from our amazing mom and dad. That shared passion inspired us to create a café where we could bring together the things we love most.

For us, Jade & Janes is about more than just coffee and baked goods. It's about family, spending time together, and creating a welcoming space where everyone feels at home.

What Makes Us Special

At Jade & Janes, we believe great coffee and baked goods start with quality ingredients and a little extra love.

  • Fresh Sourdough: Our breads and baked goods are made with sourdough, bringing homemade flavor to every bite.
  • Homemade Syrups: We create our own coffee syrups and sauces to give our drinks a special touch.
  • Locally Roasted Coffee: We support local coffee roasters and serve handcrafted espresso drinks.
  • Something for Everyone: We strive to offer gluten-free and vegan options so more people can enjoy our treats.
